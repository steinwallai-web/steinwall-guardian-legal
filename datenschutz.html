In plain terms

Everything you submit to Guardian — pasted text, uploaded files, and, once connected, your own incoming platform messages — is analyzed by AI models running on your own device and stored encrypted, on your own device. We do not run our own cloud servers that receive your message content, we do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

1

Where your data lives

All analyzed content and case records are encrypted and stored on your own device. The encryption key is derived from your password and a recovery phrase generated only for you; it is never written to disk in plain form and never leaves your device.

We do not operate a central database of user content. There is nothing for us to hand over in a data breach at "our servers," because your case data does not live on servers we run.

2

How the analysis works

Text, image, video-frame, and voice analysis all run through AI models installed locally on your device (via Ollama and faster-whisper). Nothing you submit for analysis is sent to OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, or any other third-party cloud AI provider.

3

The TikTok connection, specifically

If you choose to connect your TikTok account, Guardian uses TikTok's official Data Portability API (available to EEA and UK users under the EU Digital Markets Act) to request your own incoming direct messages — date, sender, and content — on an ongoing basis, solely to screen them for harassment, threats, and fraud.

  • Guardian only ever requests your own inbox — never another person's account.
  • Guardian never posts, replies, or sends anything on TikTok on your behalf; the connection is read-only.
  • You can revoke the connection at any time, from Guardian or from your TikTok account settings, and revoking it stops all further access immediately.
  • Retrieved messages are processed and stored the same way as any other analyzed content: locally, encrypted, under your key.
4

Sharing and sale of data

We do not sell your data. We do not share message content with advertisers, data brokers, or any third party. We do not use your content to train AI models. The only data leaving your device is what is strictly necessary to retrieve content you've asked Guardian to fetch (for example, the TikTok API calls described above), and reports you explicitly choose to export and share yourself.

5

Retention and deletion

Case records are kept until you delete them. Deleting a case removes it from your encrypted local store immediately. Deleting your account removes your local data entirely; because encryption keys are not stored anywhere outside your device, this is not reversible by us.

6

Your rights

Because your data is stored locally under your own key, you already hold direct access, export, and deletion controls inside Guardian itself. If you are in the EEA or UK and have questions about your rights under GDPR in connection with the TikTok connection specifically, contact us at the address below.

7

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top of this page will be updated to reflect the latest revision.
