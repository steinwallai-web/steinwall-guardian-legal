Guardian analyzes messages you choose to share with it — pasted text, screenshots, or (once you connect it) your own incoming platform messages — to flag harassment, threats, and scams. 1

What Guardian is

STEINWALL Guardian is a personal digital-safety tool. It examines text, images, short video clips, and voice messages that you submit to it, and — where you have explicitly connected a third-party account — messages sent to that account, in order to identify harassment, threats, and fraud attempts directed at you. Analysis runs on local AI models on your own device; nothing you submit is sent to a third-party cloud AI service.

Guardian is a detection and documentation aid. It is not a law firm, does not provide legal advice, and does not file reports with authorities or platforms on your behalf.

2

Who can use it

You must be able to form a binding agreement under the law of your country of residence to use Guardian. If you are connecting a third-party account (such as TikTok), you must be the legitimate holder of that account and old enough to hold it under that platform's own terms.

3

Your account

Guardian currently operates as a single local account per installation, secured by a password you choose and a one-time recovery phrase generated at setup. You are responsible for keeping both safe. If you lose both your password and your recovery phrase, Guardian cannot recover your encrypted data — this is a deliberate design choice, not an oversight, and is disclosed to you at account creation.

4

Connecting third-party accounts

Where Guardian offers a connection to a third-party platform (for example, TikTok's official Data Portability API), the connection is established through that platform's own sign-in and consent screen. Guardian only requests access to your own incoming messages on that account — never another person's account, and never the ability to post, message, or take any action on your behalf.

You can revoke this access at any time, either from within Guardian or directly from the third-party platform's own settings.

5

What the analysis does and doesn't do

Analysis results (category, risk level, a risk score, and a plain-language explanation) are generated by an AI model and are estimates, not certainties. Guardian can miss genuine threats and can occasionally flag harmless content. Always use your own judgment, and contact the relevant platform or the authorities directly for anything urgent.

  • Guardian does not verify the identity of anyone mentioned in analyzed content.
  • Guardian does not automatically report, block, or reply to anyone.
  • A "case" record you export (as text or PDF) is a summary you generate — you decide who to share it with and when.

Tiers and cost

Guardian is currently free to use. No payment is currently collected for any tier, and no tier grants access beyond what is described in this document.

Acceptable use

Use Guardian only to protect yourself or someone who has asked you to help protect them. Do not use it to monitor another adult without their knowledge or consent, to build a case against someone for purposes unrelated to your own safety, or to submit content you do not have the right to analyze.
